**Tweet 1 — what it is / why Midnight**

Payroll is the perfect use case for blockchains — trustless, no
intermediary, auditable — and the worst fit for a *public* chain, because
everyone's exact pay becomes visible forever.

QuietPay fixes that: fund a pool publicly, pay everyone privately. Built
on @MidnightNtwrk.

---

**Tweet 2 — technical insight on the privacy model**

The trick isn't hiding numbers — it's separating two questions that
usually get bundled together:

"Did the payout happen correctly?" → public, auditable.
"Who got how much?" → private, provable only by the recipient.

`disclose()` in Compact lets a contract answer the first question loudly
and the second one never.
